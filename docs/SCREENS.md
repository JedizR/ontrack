# Ontrack: screens and flows

Each screen lists what it contains from top to bottom, its states, and its words. Words are quoted from `src/lib/i18n/en.ts`, which is the truth; layout values come from `docs/DESIGN.md`. `/gallery/today` is the worked example of how a spec here becomes a screen. Section numbers are cited from code comments, so they stay as they are.

## 1. Map

~~~
First run:  Landing page > Onboarding (up to 10 questions) > Building your plan > Your plan > Sign in > Today
Returning:  Landing page > Sign in > Today

Dock:       Today | Train | Progress, and the plus, which opens Add
Header:     the tab's title, the sync mark, the map button (signed in), the person button
Person:     Profile > Settings (a button at the foot of Profile)
Map:        the food map, with the friends panel over it (from the header, or Profile's Friends box)
Sheets:     Sign in, Add (camera, Describe, Search, Quick add, Photos, Saved meals), Review, Meal, Scan,
            Supplement, Weigh in, How this was calculated, Session menu, Session, Exercise picker,
            Programs, Adjust with AI, Check-in, Ask Ontrack, History, Edit profile, Settings and its pages
~~~

## 2. Shell

- **Header**, sticky, on every tab. The title is the tab's name, or the day with its three-letter weekday ("Tue, Nov 10") when Today shows a past day. On the right: the sync mark, the map button, the person button.
  - Sync mark: hidden when idle, a pulsing dot while syncing, a hollow ring offline, the attention colour when something needs attention; a tap says which in a toast (`t.sync`).
  - Map button, a 44 round button: only when signed in and the data is ready. A violet badge counts friend requests waiting ("2 friend requests" to assistive tech). It opens the food map (section 13).
  - Person button, labelled "Profile": opens Profile (section 11). Signed out it is "Sign in" and opens the sign in sheet.
- **Dock**: a floating pill with three tabs and the plus to its right, an ink circle. A dot on Progress while the weekly check-in is ready (not while Progress is open). Before the data is ready the plus opens sign in. Sheets sit over the dock.
- Swipe left or right moves between tabs and wraps round; sheets, wheels and horizontal scrollers do not trigger it. A tab stays mounted once opened. Changing tab returns Today to today.
- **Keyboard.** iOS overlays the keyboard on the layout viewport, so `keyboard.ts` publishes `--kb` and `--vvh` while a field has focus; sheets and the onboarding bar sit `--kb` above the bottom. Not typing, both are unset.
- On screens wider than 480 px the app is a centred column.
- **Styles.** Tray is the default: white shells holding grey boxes (nested radii 48, 40, 32), a glass header capsule once content scrolls under it, a glass dock, glass chips. Bento is flat: no card in a card, one radius 24, no glass, a plain header bar with a hairline once content scrolls under it, a solid dock whose selected tab is a black pill. Picked in Settings (section 11) or on Today's one-time card. The landing page is always Tray. This section is the one place the styles are compared; later sections say so only where the words or order differ.

## 3. Landing page

The first screen for a signed out visitor with no onboarding draft (PRD F18). It also shows when a visitor comes back from the first question, and for a signed in account that has no plan yet (its Sign in switches accounts). A returning user (the phone knows its last user) and a visitor with a draft skip it. Ontrack runs in the browser tab; nothing mentions installing. A lazy chunk of its own; onboarding loads beside it, so Build your plan answers at once. Words in `t.landing`.

From the top:
- **Bar.** Fixed, 8 below the safe area: a Liquid Glass capsule 52 high with the ONTRACK wordmark and "Sign in" (an ink pill nested 8 inside), which opens the sign in sheet titled "Welcome back". Over the hero's sky it has no glass; once content scrolls under it the glass grows out of Sign in, dark over the dark sections.
- **Hero.** On the sky gradient: "Eat for the body you are training for." in Poster capitals, "you are training for." in violet; "Your daily calories and protein, from published equations and how you train. Raised on training days, and checked against your weigh-ins every week."; the primary "Build your plan" with "No account until you save it." under it. Below it (beside it on a wide screen), Today in a drawn iPhone, with two tilted glass chips: "Training day" and "On track".
- **Statement.** On a white sheet that rises over the hero at radius 48: "Ontrack builds your target from [how you train], logs a meal from [one photo], and corrects it from [your own weigh-ins] every week." in Statement size, the bracketed words in pills (the last one ink), four tilted violet icon tiles around it.
- **01 Logging.** "One photo logs a meal." and a lead on where calories come from. Four cards, each a title, a line and a real screen: Snap the plate (the one solid violet card), Scan the barcode, Thai food by name, Shakes by the scoop.
- **02 Training.** A dark rounded section: "Your training sets the target." Two dark cards with flat palette panels, a violet icon tile and a number badge: More food on training days, A floor for lifters who struggle to gain.
- **03 Check-in.** "Corrected every week." A lavender panel with rings, the check-in in a phone and two tilted glass cards in the check-in's own words. Beside it a 1-2-3 stepper: Weigh in, Log your meals, Choose your next target.
- **04 Friends.** "Your friends' meals, on a map." The food map in a phone, whole so its friends panel shows, beside three points with violet icon tiles: Friends by request, Off until you share, Rounded, never tracked.
- **05 Sources.** The dark run to the end starts here. "Every number shows its work." Four colour-washed cards, each a part of the target and its source, read from the engine's constants: resting energy, training energy, gain pace, Thai dishes.
- **Closing call.** "Plan. Log. Adjust." in Poster capitals, a violet panel with the mark, "Your calories and protein for training days and rest days, from how you train.", the white "Build your plan" and its caption. Under it, "Already have an account? Sign in".
- **Footer.** "Keep your calories" in Poster capitals over the wordmark, both the same width; under a hairline the wellness disclaimer centred ("Ontrack gives general wellness guidance, not medical advice."), "Ontrack 2026" at the left, "Version" and the build's number at the right, and the contact and legal links (the support address, Privacy, Terms) first on a phone.
- **Floating action.** Once the hero's Build your plan has scrolled away, the same ink pill floats 16 above the bottom, never at the edge, where Safari would take its colour for its bar. It stays away from the closing call to the end.

Every phone shows a real screen of the app, made by `tests/e2e/landing-shots.spec.ts`; the alt text says what each shows. No testimonials, ratings, counts, logos, prices or claims beyond what the app does. Images load lazily below the hero. With reduced motion everything shows at rest at once.

## 4. Onboarding

Ask only what moves the number or a guardrail, default the rest in the open, build the number in front of the user.

Full height on the landing page's sky (a light island in both themes, Safari's top edge tinted with it). A thin track at the top with "Step n of N", where N counts only the questions this route asks (it can shrink as answers drop a question, never grow). **Back sits at the bottom**: a small white button with an ink chevron beside the main action, both in the landing page's Liquid Glass capsule. Everything arrives in order with the landing page's blur settle. One question per screen, the question in Title, its caption in Lead, the answers in the middle.

A tap selects an answer and never moves on: the picked card fills ink, its icon tile turns light lavender, a ring at its right fills with a white check, and the answer is saved, so the number so far grows on that tap. Continue moves on. Until there is an answer Continue has a faint tint (aria-disabled, not `disabled`), and a tap on it shakes the answers left right left, with a buzz and no words.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What are you training for? | Three cards with icon tiles: Build muscle (Gain steadily.), Lose fat (Keep your muscle.), Maintain (Eat to match training.). Under them a small violet link, "Other goals", shows two more and moves focus to the first: Gain weight (Gain faster if you are lean.) and Lose fat and build muscle (A small deficit, more protein.) | The two map onto engine goals (SCIENCE.md section 6, vectors J and K). Back on this step with one of them picked shows all five |

| 9 | What do you train? | Lifting (Weights or bodyweight), Cardio (Running, cycling, swimming), Both (Taking turns, lifting first) | Caption "Sport counts as cardio." Sets the sessions: lifting an hour, cardio half an hour, Both taking turns with lifting first, all at a moderate effort |

Steps are numbered as in the longest route; 2's advice screen, 6, 9 and 10 are asked only when their answers call for them.

**So far.** From the daily movement question on, the pinned footer holds a card above the capsule: Eyebrow "So far", under it the latest row with what it added ("Daily movement +315 kcal"), the value in Heading with "kcal" on the right. It shows resting energy first, adds daily movement, then a training day's exercise energy once the days and the kind are known. Each value comes from the engine with the goal held at maintain, so it is the number the breakdown later shows. It never appears before screening is answered and the weight is known. Neither violet nor green; announced politely.

**Building your plan.** After the last question's Continue, about 4 seconds: a ring on the sky fills as a percent counts up in it, and five rows tick off one a beat, each with a haptic: "Daily energy use", "Training day targets" (with no training, "One daily target"), "Protein for your goal", "Safe pace", "Sources for each number". Then the ring becomes a violet check, the title changes from "Building your plan" to "Your plan is ready", and Your plan follows. It plays once a visit, and under reduced motion too (the pacing and the count are not motion).

**Your plan.** Computed before Building your plan shows.
- A doctor card first when BMI is under 16 ("See a doctor first").
- Hero: the training day target in Hero style, counting up from zero, "kcal on training days", and "2,220 on rest days" under it (one number, "kcal a day", in even mode). Under it, Protein, Carbs and Fat in grams on three tiles, each counting up 150 ms after the one before. Tray: the number sits on the haze panel in a white shell; Bento: on a white box.
- **Goal weight**: a row with its hint "Optional. Does not change your target.", the goal or "None yet", a chevron and the wheel sheet with Clear.
- **Pace** (lose and gain, not for a screening flag): a segmented control of the paces the guardrails allow (Gentle, Steady, Faster, or Lean, Standard, Faster), the default from SCIENCE.md picked. Under it: "Expect to gain about 0.3 kg a week. This is a starting estimate. Ontrack corrects it from your results after two weeks." A missing pace has one line saying why (BMI limits; three years of lifting; for gain at BMI 20 or more, "The faster pace is offered below a BMI of 20."). The faster gain pace adds "A faster gain, with more of it as fat than at the lean pace." and "Being checked". Gain weight starts on the faster pace where it is offered. Lose fat and build muscle has no pace control: "The gentle pace with a cut's protein, so muscle can still grow while you lift."
- One card of three disclosures, closed until asked for: "Your training" ("4 sessions a week", inside "Lifting 4 days a week, 60 min each. Edit in Train."), "How this was calculated" (resting energy, daily movement, training and the goal adjustment, summing to the target, each line opening its source and status: Working assumption, Being checked) and "Fine tune" (Body fat, "Same target every day").
- Any flag in one line in the attention colour ("We limited the deficit to 25 percent of your maintenance").
- Each block below the hero settles in as it scrolls into view; without motion it is just there.
- Primary "Save this plan", beside Back. Signed out, "Next: sign in with Google or an email code." ends the page. Signed in, the plan is written at once.

Answers live in the prefs draft, so a reload or the Google redirect lands on the same screen. After sign in the draft becomes the plan, or the account's own plan wins and the draft is discarded.

**What the answers change after onboarding.**
- Train: while the program lists no exercises, a card (eyebrow "Suggested for your week") names the template that fits the days, the kind and the experience as its title, "Upper lower 4 days", then "Fits 4 days a week." and who it suits, with "Use this program" and "Not now". A gain goal with cardio only, or no training, is offered Minimal 2 days for its two strength sessions.
- Add: a lifter can set up whey or creatine from the pinned pills. For a gain goal the supplement kind list leads with whey and mass gainer. Ontrack never recommends a supplement.
- Search: when the phone's languages include Thai, Thai dishes rank first among equal matches, and Describe's example is a Thai meal.

## 5. Sign in

A sheet over Today or the plan, or over the landing page for returning users. The title is "Save your plan" from Your plan, else "Welcome back".
- Lead: "Your plan stays in this browser and backs up to your account for your other devices."
- "Continue with Google" as a neutral button with the Google mark, only where the project has Google on; then a hairline with "Or".
- Email field; "We email you a 6 digit code. Type it here, even if you open the email on another device." Pinned primary "Send code" ("Sending").
- Code step: "Enter the 6 digit code we sent to name@example.com." Six boxes over one real input, so iOS fills the code from Mail and a paste lands whole; the sixth digit checks it. Pinned "Sign in" ("Checking the code"). "Send again in 24 s", then "Send again" ("We sent a new code"); "Use another email".
- Errors, in the danger colour under the field: "That code did not match. Check the email and try again." (shake, clear), "That code has expired. Send a new one." (after 10 minutes), "You are offline. Connect to the internet to sign in.", "Too many tries for now. Wait a few minutes, then send a new code.", "Enter an email address, like name@example.com.", "Sign in did not work. Try again in a moment."
- Small print: "By signing in you accept the Terms and the Privacy notice." with both as links.

## 6. Today

Matches `/gallery/today`. Header "Today" (section 2). From the top:

1. **Week strip.** Today is a chip pinned at the right, wearing an ink ring while another day is in view; the days before it, eight weeks of them, slide six at a time and lock to their slots when a finger lifts. Each shows its weekday in one letter, the date, and a dot at its foot for an on track day. The three left slots lean left: tapping one slides it to the fourth slot, the middle of the row (as far as the ends allow); tapping Today slides to the newest days and picks today. Days leave under a soft edge before the Today chip, none while yesterday is picked. A past day changes the header title to its date.
2. **The day card**, one shell (Tray) holding:
   - **Hero**, a button on the haze panel, opening the sheet "How this was calculated" for the day in view: "For a training day" (or "For a rest day", "Every day, training averaged"), then resting energy, daily movement, training and the goal adjustment, summing to the target, each line opening its source and status (also reached from Settings). Calories eaten in Hero style counting up, "of 2,460 kcal" under it. On the right a glass chip, "Training day" or "Rest day", and the counterpart: what is left as a number with its caption ("620" and "kcal to go" for a gain goal, "kcal left" for lose and maintain). Inside the on track zone it becomes a green check and "On track", the amount in the caption ("8 kcal left"). Past the target all goals show "240" and "kcal over" in plain ink, never red. Under it the track.
   - **Macro boxes**: Protein, Carbs and Fat, each a grey box with the eaten grams over "/ 150 g" and a track at its foot; Protein shows a green check while inside its band.
   - **Training and weigh-in**, two thirds and one third. Each session of the day is its own box with its own Done: its title (a session named by its kind shows its machine, "Treadmill", with its kind under it), then the facts "Strength | 60 min | -240 kcal" and a primary Done pill. With several sessions the box keeps its size after Done and the facts sit at its foot; a single session centres its words. Tapping the title opens the Session menu sheet: "Done" and "Skip today".
     - Done: the box turns ink, spreading from the button; a green pill with a check pops in with a subtle burst, carrying the burn counting down from 0 to its negative ("-240"); Done becomes Undo, a grey pill on the ink, which plays it back. Skipped shows "Skipped. The day uses your rest target" and a plain Undo. A done training day keeps its higher target.
     - A day with no session shows a box "Rest day", or "Add your training to sharpen your target" when there is no program.
     - **Weigh-in box**: a scale, "Weigh in", and how long since the last ("3 days ago", "Yesterday", "Today", or "Your first"), with Add. Weighed in, the box turns ink the same way, a green check over the weight and "Weighed in", with Edit. On a past day with none it says "No weigh-in" and offers Add. Add and Edit open the wheel sheet "Weigh in" in the user's unit, starting at the last weigh-in, with "One a day. A new one replaces it." and "Save 82.4 kg"; the toast says "Weigh-in saved, 82.4 kg". Under 620 high the check sits beside the weight and the second line goes.

It tunes your target from your meals and weigh-ins.", a cross at the top right that hides it on this phone (toast "Guide hidden" with Undo), and three rows, each a grey icon tile that turns into a green check when done:
   - "Log a meal", "A photo, a sentence or a barcode", with Add, which opens Add.
   - "Weigh in": on the plan's first day "Tomorrow morning, before you eat" and no button (onboarding's weigh-in is that day's); after it "In the morning, before you eat" with Add, which opens the weigh-in.
   - "Mark a workout done": "Tap Done on Strength once you've trained" on a training day (Done stays on the hero, never repeated), "Next: Strength on Friday" otherwise, or "Add your training first" with Add, which opens Train.
   A done step loses its line. When the last lands, the card reads "You're all set" and "Keep logging. Your first check-in is Wednesday, Nov 25." for the rest of that day, then is gone. Each step is read from the records (any meal but a supplement with no calories, a weigh-in after the first day, any session marked Done, a rest day's activity included; a last step saved to a past day still shows all set while Today is open), so the guide matches on every device. While it asks for the first meal, the empty meal list says "Log a meal with the plus button" and the nothing logged card stays away.
   Then, once per phone, the **style card** (eyebrow "New", "Pick your style", "Ontrack comes in two looks. Tap one and the app changes right away.", two drawn tiles, Tray "Cards on a tray, with glass" and Bento "Flat boxes, no glass", the primary "Use Tray", and "Change it any time: tap your profile at the top right, then Settings."). ## 7. Add flow

**Add sheet**, from the plus. Title "Add a meal". The supplements as pills on top, then the camera and the ways to log, then re-logging what you usually eat.

1. **Pinned supplements** (R5, the owner): the pill "Whey" (a shaker icon), then every other supplement set up as its own pill (the row scrolls sideways past two), and a round plus that sets up any other, creatine included. Creatine is never pinned (R7, the owner): it is logged from the plus, whose kind list opens creatine, once set up, at its amount. Set up, a pill shows its last amount ("2 scoops with milk", "Typical" when on typical values) and its calories, and one tap opens the Supplement sheet's amount step. Not set up, one tap opens its setup at the source step (the kind is already known).
2. **The composer**, on the landing page's sky (R5, the owner's layout):
   - **The camera**, one in-app camera in a dark 3 by 4 viewfinder (the phone camera's own field, cropped to exactly what it shows), its height the width left over and at most 38 percent of the screen, with **Photos**, **Search** and **Quick add** stacked as round glass actions on its right. Under it the switch "Meal | Label" (Meal first) across the full width, then the shutter row as the iPhone camera has it (R7, the owner): the white shutter, "Take photo", centred (a white ring round a white disc), a white round **macro** button on its left (a flower, slashed while off: "Use the macro lens") where the phone has an ultra-wide back lens, found by its name, and a white round **light** button on its right (black line icon) where the camera has a light. Macro opens the ultra-wide, which focuses close, framed at 2x; if it will not open the main camera comes back without the toggle. **Zoom chips** "1×", "2×", "3×" sit in a glass pill at the foot of the picture, the chosen one white and showing the exact zoom ("1.4×"); a drag along them or a pinch on the picture zooms anywhere between, up to 3x (the camera's own zoom where the browser offers it, else the picture is scaled and the shot cropped to match). One finger on the picture still scrolls the sheet. A small glass chip "Reads barcodes", its dot pulsing while live; a barcode is read in either mode, by itself, with a light haptic, and wins at once. No frame is drawn. The hint rides on the picture, over the zoom chips: "Starting the camera", "Frame the whole plate.", "Fill the camera with the label.".
   - **Meal**: Take photo keeps the frame as a JPEG and stops the camera, and Review opens with the photo.
   - **Label**: Take photo hands the frame to the Scan sheet, which reads it ("Reading the label").
   - A barcode hands to the Scan sheet, which finds the product.
   - The camera cannot open: the hint says why ("Camera access is off for Ontrack. Type the number, or allow the camera for this site in your browser's settings.", "This browser cannot open a camera here. Type the number instead.", "Another app is using the camera. Close it and try again, or type the number.") and a "Type the number" button appears (with "Try again" when the camera is busy). Typing swaps the viewfinder for a "Barcode number" field and "Find product", with a back chevron to the camera.
   - Last, under the shutter: a pill field whose example line turns every 3 seconds ("Two eggs, toast with butter and a latte"; Thai first on a Thai phone) opens Describe; a mic button beside it opens Describe already listening. Photos is the phone's photo library or camera, then Review.
3. **Other supplements**, when set up: under the eyebrow "Supplements", one row per supplement with the shaker tile, the last amount as its caption and its calories (none for a supplement with no calories), opening the amount step; under them a plain "Add a supplement".

## 12. System states

- **Offline:** the sync mark is hollow. Nothing else changes except what needs the network (photo analysis, the coach, the map, friends), which says so.
- **Update ready:** a toast, "A new version is ready", with "Update".
- **Session expired:** a card on Today (section 6) that opens the sign in sheet. Local data is untouched.
- **First load on a new device after sign in:** a skeleton of Today with "Bringing your data over".
- **Every destructive action:** an undo toast when it can be undone, a typed or stepped confirmation when it cannot (Remove friend, Delete account).
