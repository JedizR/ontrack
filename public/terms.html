Terms of use

Ontrack is a class project: a proof of concept of a calorie and training tracker, made for a course and not launched to the public. It is free. By using it, you agree to these terms.

Not medical advice

Ontrack gives general wellness guidance, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or a dietitian before you make a big change to how you eat or train, and stop and get help if something feels wrong.

Who it is for

  • Adults, 18 and over.
  • If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a medical condition or take medication that affects your diet, Ontrack recommends setting targets with your doctor or a dietitian, and offers a maintenance target only.
  • If you have, or have had, an eating disorder, Ontrack advises against counting calories without professional support, and offers to end the setup. If your care team agrees, it offers a maintenance target only.
  • At a low body weight, Ontrack does not offer weight loss, and at a very low one it suggests seeing a doctor.

How your targets are worked out

Your targets come from published equations and studies. Each one is listed with its source, and with how sure it is, on the app's The science page in Settings. Every target is an estimate: people differ from what general equations predict. Ontrack corrects your target from your own weigh-ins and log at the check-in.

What the AI does

When you analyse a photo, a label or a description, an AI model names the foods and estimates the grams. That is all it does. It never sets your targets. Calories, protein, carbs and fat come from food tables whenever there is a match, and an item without one is marked Estimate. The AI can be wrong, so check what it found before you save it.

Food data

Food values come from the USDA FoodData Central tables, SG FoodID from Singapore's Health Promotion Board, the Thai Food Composition Database from INMU at Mahidol University, and, for scanned barcodes, Open Food Facts, which is open data under the Open Database License. These sources can hold mistakes, and a product's label can differ from them.

Your account and what you share

  • Keep your sign in to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • What you log stays yours. You let Ontrack store it and process it only to run the app for you, and to show your friends what you choose to share.
  • Share only photos you have the right to share, and nothing harmful, hateful or private about other people. Do not use a handle or name that pretends to be someone else.
  • Do not try to break into the service, read other people's data, or overload it.

Use at your own risk

Ontrack is offered as it is, without any promise that it is accurate, complete or always available. It runs on free plans with daily limits, so photo analysis can be busy at times. As far as the law allows, the owner is not liable for any loss or harm from using it.

Changes and ending

The owner can change these terms, change Ontrack, or pause or stop it at any time. Where possible, the owner will give notice first, so you can export your data from Settings. You can stop using Ontrack and delete your account at any time.
